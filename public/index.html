About this map

Live buses for Cincinnati Metro and TANK (Northern Kentucky), drawn over neighborhood, city and township boundaries. Not an official app and not affiliated with or endorsed by SORTA/Metro, TANK, or any government listed below. Positions and predictions can be wrong or late: don't rely on this for trip planning.

How it was made

  • Written with Claude Code (Anthropic) as a p5.js sketch, the JavaScript successor to Processing.js.
  • A small Node.js server with no dependencies fetches the transit feeds (they can't be read from a browser directly), decodes GTFS-realtime protobufs with a hand-written reader, unpacks the timetable zips, and caches boundary data in data/.
  • Lateness is the predicted arrival at the next stop minus the scheduled time. Metro's comes from its trip-update predictions against the timetable; TANK's from the delay its feed reports.
  • Time lost is history this server records itself: every 30 s it notes how much each bus's delay changed and where, keeps 24 hours, and sums it into ~220 m grid cells. Nearest stop names come from the timetables, which also mark route ends (where layovers happen) and timepoints (where early buses hold).
  • Bunching: two buses on the same route, heading the same way, within 500 m of each other.

Data sources

  • Cincinnati Metro (SORTA): GTFS timetable and GTFS-realtime vehicle positions, trip updates and alerts. Developer data.
    License: SORTA/Metro developer data license: non-exclusive, limited and revocable rights to use, reproduce and redistribute. Provided "as is" without warranty. SORTA/Metro keeps ownership, and its trademarks may not be used with the data without written approval.
  • TANK (Transit Authority of Northern Kentucky): GTFS timetable and GTFS-realtime feeds from its InfoPoint rider site (Avail Technologies). tankbus.org.
    License: offered free to developers; no written license was found. Rights remain with TANK.
  • Cincinnati neighborhoods: City of Cincinnati / CAGIS (Cincinnati Area Geographic Information System), neighborhoods layer.
    Terms: the CAGIS data disclaimer: provided "as is", with no warranty and no liability for defects.
  • Covington neighborhoods: City of Covington, neighborhoods layer on ArcGIS Online (see also CovData).
    License: none stated on the layer. Used from the public service with credit.
  • Northern Kentucky city limits: Commonwealth of Kentucky, KY city limits via KyGeoNet. Created by the Kentucky Transportation Cabinet and updated by the Division of Geographic Information. Boone, Kenton and Campbell County boundaries come from the Northern Kentucky Area Planning Commission.
    License: none stated on the layer. Used from the public service with credit.
  • Ohio cities, villages and townships; Kentucky communities; counties; Ohio River: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb (places, county subdivisions, census-designated places, counties, hydrography).
    License: public domain (a U.S. government work, 17 U.S.C. § 105).
